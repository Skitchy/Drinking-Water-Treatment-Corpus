## Candidate challenge questions
- Q: By what date must Subpart H systems serving 10,000 or more persons comply with subpart L requirements?
  A: January 1, 2002
- Q: By what date must Subpart H systems serving fewer than 10,000 persons, or systems using only ground water not under the direct influence of surface water, comply with subpart L?
  A: January 1, 2004
- Q: During a distribution line break, may a system raise the chlorine dioxide residual above its MRDL to protect public health?
  A: No. Only chlorine or chloramine residuals may be increased above the MRDL for this purpose; chlorine dioxide is explicitly excluded.
- Q: What disinfection byproducts does EPA name as examples under this section?
  A: Chloroform; bromodichloromethane; dibromochloromethane; bromoform; dichloroacetic acid; and trichloroacetic acid.
- Q: Who is required to operate a CWS or NTNCWS regulated under this section?
  A: Qualified personnel who meet State-specified requirements and are included in a State register of qualified operators.
