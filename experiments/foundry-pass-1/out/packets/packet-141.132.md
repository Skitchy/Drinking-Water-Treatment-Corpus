## Candidate challenge questions
- Q: How often must a Subpart H system serving at least 10,000 persons sample for TTHM and HAA5 under routine monitoring?
  A: Four water samples per quarter per treatment plant.
- Q: What source water TOC level qualifies a Subpart H system serving at least 10,000 persons for reduced TTHM/HAA5 monitoring?
  A: A source water annual average TOC level, before any treatment, of ≤4.0 mg/L (along with TTHM ≤0.040 mg/L and HAA5 ≤0.030 mg/L).
- Q: What TTHM and HAA5 levels must a system stay at or below to remain on a reduced monitoring schedule?
  A: No more than 0.060 mg/L for TTHM and 0.045 mg/L for HAA5.
- Q: Can chlorine dioxide monitoring at the entrance to the distribution system ever be reduced?
  A: No, chlorine dioxide monitoring may not be reduced.
- Q: What bromate running annual average allows a system to reduce monitoring from monthly to quarterly beginning April 1, 2009?
  A: A running annual average bromate concentration of ≤0.0025 mg/L, based on monthly measurements for the most recent four quarters.
- Q: At what population threshold must a Subpart H system submit a copy of its monitoring plan to the State?
  A: Systems serving more than 3300 people.
- Q: What treated water TOC average allows a Subpart H system to reduce TOC and alkalinity monitoring to quarterly?
  A: An average treated water TOC of less than 2.0 mg/L for two consecutive years, or less than 1.0 mg/L for one year.
