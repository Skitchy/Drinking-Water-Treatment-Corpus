- Q: By what date must a Subpart H system serving fewer than 10,000 persons, or a system using only ground water not under the direct influence of surface water, comply with Subpart L?
  A: January 1, 2004
- Q: Which disinfection byproducts are named as examples that TTHM/HAA5 MCLs and DBP precursor treatment technique requirements aim to limit?
  A: Chloroform, bromodichloromethane, dibromochloromethane, bromoform, dichloroacetic acid, and trichloroacetic acid
- Q: During a distribution line break, may an operator raise the chlorine dioxide residual above its MRDL to protect public health?
  A: No. Section 141.130(d) allows increasing chlorine or chloramine residuals above the MRDL for such events, but explicitly excludes chlorine dioxide.
- Q: Who is permitted to operate a CWS or NTNCWS regulated under this subpart?
  A: Only qualified personnel who meet the requirements specified by the State and are included in a State register of qualified operators.
